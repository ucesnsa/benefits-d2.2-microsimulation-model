## Settled decisions

1. **WELLBY spine.** 1 WELLBY = £13,000 (2019 prices), low £10,000, high £16,000. The £16,300 applied at 2024 prices is the same spine uprated, not a second value; the spine is always cited as £13,000 at 2019 prices The applied £16,300 IS shown on every tool face (2026-08-01), always with its derivation: showing the base alone while the arithmetic used the applied figure was the defect, not the fix. The EU spine is built from £13,000 (2019), PPP-adjusted to national price levels and uprated to 2024, and flagged a transfer; it is never built from £16,300 (no double-uprate). The price year is always carried.

2. **One-route discipline.** Each outcome is valued by exactly one route (ENGINE, WELLBY, CONVENTIONAL, COST-INPUT, or UNEVIDENCED-EXCLUDED). No outcome carries two routes; no double-counting. The employment income and employment wellbeing outcomes are distinct outcomes, each on one route.

3. **Standing exclusions (UNEVIDENCED-EXCLUDED; do not value, do not transfer).** Food aid and food banks non-income outcome (all four, including the UK); mental-health talking therapies for ES, IT, EL (the UK IAPT row stays evidenced; the UK 0.71 is not transferred); UC and income-support processing non-income "averted hardship" (all); the minimum-income claim-support hardship unit value distinct from the ENGINE income effect (all three).

4. **Domestic-abuse advocacy effect.** OR 0.43 central (physical abuse at 12 to 24 months; Ramsay / Campbell 2009); OR 0.39 the severe-physical-abuse-at-24-months bound (NNT 8; Rivas / Cochrane 2015). 0.43 is the central and 0.39 the severe bound, not the reverse.

5. **Drug route (EU): VSL times averted-mortality, not BCR times cost.** Scope caveat: the EU route values averted drug-related deaths only, whereas the UK CONVENTIONAL drug cell values the full benefit bundle (crime, health, productivity, mortality). The EU figure is a strict subset of the UK figure and is smaller by construction; the two are not like-for-like and must never be compared as equals. **Sordo classification:** Sordo et al. (2017), *BMJ* 2017;357:j1550, is an international systematic review and meta-analysis, so the mortality effect (RR 3.20 unadjusted out-to-in; related OAT all-cause RR 0.47, Santo / EUDA summary) is a flagged transfer for all three countries; only the national VSLs and OST costs are country-specific. **Baseline drug-related mortality, now recorded:** ES 38, IT 6, EL 35 drug-induced deaths per million aged 15-64 (EUDA *European Drug Report 2025*, Annex Table 6; crude, not age-standardised, under-ascertained).

6. **Housing route (EU): CONVENTIONAL averted-cost transfer, all three**, matching the UK and preserving cross-country comparability. The Hábitat result is reported in prose as a country-specific finding and is not monetised via WELLBY (it is an outlier within Housing First RCT evidence; the rescaled gain produces an implausibly high per-person value; routing the same service by WELLBY in ES and CONVENTIONAL elsewhere would break comparability). The 1-to-7 to 0-10 rescaling is dropped. The Hábitat €46.01 per person per day (2018) is an intervention cost and is not used as an averted cost.

7. **Children's social care, Italy: incidence construction.** €87,389 per new case, inferred lifetime present value (€910,412,855 divided by 10,418 new 2010 cases; Bocconi 2013, reference year 2010, 4.6% real discount), flagged inferred and not a published figure. The annual prevalence-based €130,259 is not used (wrong construction for this route). Cite Bocconi 2013 as primary, not Ferrara 2015. (Transcription note: the exact quotient is €87,388.45; the decided figure rounds it to €87,389. See PROVIDER_EVIDENCE_EU.md, Section F.)

8. **Domestic abuse, Spain: follow the primary study, do NOT sum (supersedes the earlier composite).** Source: Universidad de Alcalá / Delegación del Gobierno contra la Violencia de Género, *Impacto de la violencia de género y de la violencia sexual contra las mujeres en España (II): una valoración de sus costes en 2022* (Ministerio de Igualdad, 2024, NIPO 048-24-002-4). Central: **gender violence alone €3,015m** (option C; precise €3,014.61m), 2022, 0.23% of GDP, €64 per capita. Joint tangible upper bound (gender + sexual violence outside partnership): €4,933.22m, 2022, 0.37% of GDP, €104 per capita (Table 1). Intangible (pain and suffering, gender violence): €4,110m (precise €4,110,037,260, Table 5), carried SEPARATELY. The study estimates tangibles for 2022 but intangibles on a lifetime-from-2022 basis (different time horizons) and therefore does NOT sum them. The previously composed harm-inclusive **€9,043m is superseded** by the study's own structure; it is a project-level construction the source does not make and must never be presented as a source-native total (label any retained harm-inclusive figure an external composition).

9. **Attribution default.** 0.5 (range 0.3 to 0.7) is the tool's own SROI-style modelling assumption, not a GMCA default. The GMCA citation (v2.0 April 2014; Unit Cost Database v3.0 November 2025) is retained for the broad cost-benefit-analysis framing only, never for the 0.5 figure.

10. **Price-year base and dual display.** The common uprating base is 2024 (the UK tool already operates in 2024, so uprating the EU figures to 2024 preserves cross-tool comparability). Spain Analyst money is 2023 nominal EUR underlying, displayed uprated to 2024 EUR (Eurostat `nama_10_gdp` deflator ×1.0289; a display-layer uprate, the surface staying 2023 nominal); Spain Provider money is 2024-equivalent EUR; the 2024 display year and the 2023 income-reference-year provenance both appear on the face of the tool. The UK base figures already displayed are in 2024 prices, so their source year and their displayed price year are distinct columns and both are recorded.

11. **FRS edition.** Cite SN 9367 (End User Licence, single-year), **1st Edition, DOI `10.5255/UKDA-SN-9367-1`**. Record SN 9256 (Secure Access cumulative) as the alternative. FRS 2023-24 released 27 March 2025. The 1st Edition is the one this build used, established by date rather than from a delivery record: the microdata were loaded and extracted on 2026-05-07, and the 2nd Edition (`10.5255/UKDA-SN-9367-2`) was not registered until 2026-05-22.

12. **Citation corrections (applied throughout, consistent with the corrected .tex and .bib).** Pleasence and Balmer 2007, "Changing Fortunes: Results from a Randomized Trial of the Offer of Debt Advice in England and Wales", *Journal of Empirical Legal Studies* 4(3):651-673 (published spelling "Randomized" retained for the title). MaPS, "Money and Pensions Service-funded debt advice impact report 2023/24". Nurmatov 2020, "Impact of shared decision-making family meetings on children's out-of-home care...", null finding. Crisis £20,128, derived 12-month rough-sleeper vignette, not a population mean. Domestic abuse £34,015, derived per-victim, 2016/17, not the uprated variants. Drug BCR: Black's 5.1 is Year 5 of a five-year plan; near-term band 2.3 / 2.5 / 4.0; £4 to £1 drug-specific, £3 to £1 alcohol. Child maltreatment: Bocconi 2013 (reference year 2010) primary, Ferrara 2015 secondary.

13. **Employment WELLBY (+0.46 retained, relabelled).** The employment effect is +0.46 WELLBY, retained, as a **Britain fixed-effects unemployment-to-employment WELLBY transfer** (the symmetric use of the approximately -0.46 unemployment coefficient, full-time employment the reference category): Clark, Flèche, Layard, Powdthavee and Ward, *The Origins of Happiness* (Princeton University Press, 2018), Table 4.2; corroborated by the New Zealand Treasury Public Benefit Exchange technical annex (0.46, 95% CI 0.38 to 0.54, attributed to Clark et al. Tables 4.1 and 4.2). Applied to all three countries as a flagged UK/EU transfer. The **"years 2 to 5" framing is removed as unverifiable** (Table 4.2 is a cross-sectional labour-force-status coefficient, not a dynamic path). The **0.7 WELLBY figure (Layard et al. 2020) was considered and rejected** on estimand grounds (it is the psychic cost of a year of unemployment, not the transition gain). (Attribution note: the brief wrote "Clark, Frijters and Layard"; the author list here follows the verified references.bib entry.)

## Settled figures, and the source each rests on

- **IT OST per-capita cost (attribution corrected).** EMCDDA *Insights 24* (2017), **Chapter 7 (Bergamo case study), Table 7.8, SERT rows** — per-capita total excluding inpatient €3,654.6 (2013) and €3,900.0 (2014); including inpatient €26,270.6 (2013) and €25,949.0 (2014). Both scope variants recorded. Corrects the prior attribution to Chapter 8 (Genetti et al.). Documentary under the VSL drug route.
- **IT primary-care GP cost.** Primary figure approximately €12 per ambulatory visit (2003): Garattini et al. (2003), DYSCO, *Farmeconomia* 4(2):109-114 (native 2003). €15.17 is dropped (not primary; a downstream CPI-inflation of €12 to 2016 by Dal Negro et al. 2016); uprate €12 in-house instead.
- **EL primary-care GP cost.** €10 retired as wrong-basis (capitation or reimbursement, not economic cost per visit; the Tsiantou capitation wording not independently re-verified, and a separate €10-per-visit EOPYY 2014 figure existed, so the basis is easily confused). The Greek GP cell is a flagged transfer from the Spanish €14.78 (2008), zero value-added (cost-input route).
- **GBV advocacy odds ratio.** OR 0.43 central (Ramsay / Campbell 2009); OR 0.39 severe-physical-abuse-at-24-months bound, NNT 8 (Rivas / Cochrane 2015). 0.43 central, 0.39 severe, not the reverse.
- **EL A21 income bands.** Equivalised household income bands: up to €6,000 (full), €6,001 to €10,000 (two-thirds), €10,001 to €15,000 (one-third); equivalence scale first parent 1, second parent 0.5, each dependent child 0.25. Source: OPEKA legislation page, governing chain Article 214 of Law 4512/2018 as amended by Law 4659/2020 and the implementing joint ministerial decision. (Per-child tier amounts €70/€42/€28 and €140/€84/€56 verified in the prior pass.)
- **Greek GBV transfer (ranked).** Preferred: EIGE Greece-specific extrapolation of approximately €2.4bn per year for intimate-partner violence against women (EIGE, *Combating Violence Against Women: Greece*, 2017 factsheet), flagged a transfer/extrapolation, not a Greek primary costing. EU-level anchors (EIGE 2021, doi:10.2839/23187): total GBV €366bn, violence against women €289bn, IPV against women €151.95bn. The EIGE per-victim scalar is not directly stated in the 2021 report and is not recorded as a figure.
- **National ALMP evidence (qualitative warrant only; no effect-size figure carried).** The +0.46 employment transfer is applied per country justified by national ALMP evidence, recorded as a qualitative warrant with no number: ES — Arranz et al., *Journal for Labour Market Research*, 2024 (participation and job-finding); IT — Reddito di Cittadinanza employment-neutral (Aprea, Gallo and Raitano, *Italian Economic Journal*, 2023); EL — DYPA wage subsidies positive (OECD 2024 DYPA evaluation, referenced, not independently re-retrieved). No ALMP effect-size figure is carried, because none feeds a displayed value; any future use must be verified against source first.
- **Deflator and PPP sources (identified for the uprating step; nothing uprated or PPP-adjusted).** GDP deflator: Eurostat teina110 for ES/IT/EL (AMECO comparator vintage); UK spine, UK Government / ONS *GDP deflators at market prices* collection. PPP (the £13,000 2019 spine and cross-country transfers): Eurostat purchasing power parities / price level indices (EU-internal method), OECD PPPs as the alternative.
